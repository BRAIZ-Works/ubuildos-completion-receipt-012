# Day 11 LinkedIn Carousel - Accessibility Companion

## Page 1 - The problem
Prospect qualification often disappears into a score nobody can inspect. Day 11 asks whether a qualification decision can remain visible and explainable.

## Page 2 - The model
Four explicit fields drive the bounded workflow: fit, need, timing, and next step. There is no hidden score.

## Page 3 - How it works
Supported evidence can qualify. Explicit non-fit, no-need, or out-of-window timing disqualifies with a visible reason. Missing, invalid, or contradictory evidence routes to REVIEW.

## Page 4 - Proof
Synthetic test cases exercise QUALIFIED, DISQUALIFIED, and REVIEW paths. Status and reasons remain visible and can be exported.

## Page 5 - Boundary
Synthetic data only. No CRM or enrichment connection. No predictive lead scoring. No revenue or conversion claim. No production-scale readiness claim. No confidential or regulated-data claim.

## Page 6 - Inspect it
Compare the explicit workflow with your own prospect qualification process. Day 11/30. One verified release.
